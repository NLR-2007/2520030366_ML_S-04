Four independent models. One accountable verdict.

SAGE‑FJD reads a job advert through four separate lenses — its wording, its structure, its meaning, and the posting campaign it belongs to. Each is calibrated and weighted by how reliable it has proven to be, then fused into a single score measured against a threshold that was frozen before the test data was ever opened.

    What a “no fraud signal” result does not mean

    The threshold is deliberately tuned for precision. When SAGE‑FJD flags a posting it is right about 93% of the time, but it catches only 42% of the fraud in the untouched test split. A clear result is weak evidence of legitimacy, not proof. Use this to prioritise review, never to approve.
